# StrataFlow Enterprise: Incident Response Plan
**Effective Date:** February 15, 2026
**Version:** 2.1

## 1. Incident Classification
Security incidents are classified into three severity levels to dictate the response protocol:
* **Severity 1 (Critical):** Confirmed exfiltration of Customer Data, widespread ransomware infection, or total loss of core application availability.
* **Severity 2 (High):** Targeted phishing campaigns, compromised employee credentials without evidence of data access, or localized system outages.
* **Severity 3 (Medium/Low):** Anomalous firewall blocks, isolated malware execution blocked by endpoint protection.

## 2. Notification SLAs
In the event of a confirmed Severity 1 (Critical) incident involving the unauthorized access or acquisition of Customer Data, StrataFlow Enterprise commits to notifying the primary security contact of all affected customers within **48 hours** of incident confirmation.

## 3. Response Team
The Computer Security Incident Response Team (CSIRT) is led by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). The CSIRT maintains a 24/7/365 on-call rotation utilizing PagerDuty.
